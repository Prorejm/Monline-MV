A Bad End is a specialized game over where The Player transforms into a monstergirl, effectively ending his quest. There are over 300 Bad Ends, with some as simple as losing to an enemy, and some as tricky as using an item in a specific location.

With a few exceptions, every bad end features sex and several illustrations, the brunt of them being NSFW. As such, some endings lack images to comply with Miraheze's terms of service. Hopefully the information in these entries is enough for you to see the content of the endings on your own.
